Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [PUBLISH DATE]

Welcome to Parking Sign Master (Sweden), an application provided by Sparkling AI AB, a company registered in Sweden ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"). The app appears on your phone as Sign Master. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use the Parking Sign Master (Sweden) app ("App"). Please read it to understand how we handle your data and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of data when you use the App:

a) Contact Information

  • Name (Used for App Functionality)
  • Email Address (Used for App Functionality, service messages, and, only if you opt in, parking tips by email)

b) Usage Data

  • Product Interaction (Used for Analytics & App Functionality)

c) Diagnostics Data

  • Crash Data (Used for Analytics & App Functionality)
  • Device Information: your phone's platform (iOS or Android), the App version and the operating system version, sent with each scan (Used to diagnose and fix errors)

d) Uploaded Data

  • Images of Parking Signs (Used for AI model improvement, feature testing, and bug fixes; stored temporarily for up to 12 months)

e) Communication Preferences

  • Your choice about parking tips by email, with a history of each change (see section 3); used to respect your choice and to show what you agreed to
  • App language (used to send emails in your language and to record the language you saw the request in)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide core functionality of the App, including processing parking sign images.
  • To improve AI models by using uploaded images to enhance sign recognition.
  • To process in-app purchases and manage credit-based transactions.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it, using our own records of scans and feedback.
  • To handle user authentication and account management via Clerk.
  • To process payments for credits via RevenueCat.
  • To ensure app stability by collecting crash data and diagnosing technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse (e.g., preventing exploitation of free credits).
  • To send service messages about your account, security, and important changes to this Privacy Policy or our Terms of Use.
  • To send parking tips by email before holidays, including reminders to use Sign Master, only if you have opted in.

3. Email Communications and Notifications

a) Service Messages

We may email all users about their account, security, or important changes to this Privacy Policy or our Terms of Use. These messages are not marketing and contain no offers. We send them because they are necessary to provide the App and to keep you informed.

b) Parking Tips by Email (Opt-In)

  • If you opt in, Sparkling AI AB sends you a few emails a year before holidays, with parking tips and reminders to use Sign Master. We send them only if you opt in.
  • You can opt in when you create an account with your email address (the box is not ticked by default), on a screen the App shows to users who have not made this choice yet, or at any time with the "Parking tips by email" switch in the App's Settings. On that screen, the switches start off and "Continue" saves your choices as shown. "Not now" saves nothing; the App then shows the screen once more, about three weeks before the next holiday.
  • The legal basis for these emails is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time, free of charge, using the unsubscribe link in every such email or the switch in the App's Settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect emails sent before.

c) No Open or Click Tracking

The emails we send through our email service, Brevo, both service messages and parking tips, have no open or click tracking. We do not record whether you open them or which links you click. If you use the unsubscribe link, Brevo tells us, so we can update your choice in the App.

d) Holiday and School-Break Reminders (Notifications on Your Phone)

  • If you turn them on, the App shows occasional notifications before holidays and school breaks, with parking tips and reminders to use Sign Master. They are off unless you turn them on, on the screen described above or with the "Holiday and school-break reminders" switch in the App's Settings.
  • These reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. The App does not send your choice or the reminders to us.
  • The App needs your permission to show notifications. You can turn the reminders off at any time in the App's Settings, or turn off notifications for the App in your phone's settings.

e) Where Your Choices Are Stored

  • Holiday and school-break reminders: your choice stays on your phone, and nothing about it is sent to us. It applies to that phone only, so on another phone the App asks again.
  • Parking tips by email: your choice is stored with your account on our server. For each change, we keep a history entry with the choice, the time, where you made it (when you signed up, on the screen described above, in Settings, or with the unsubscribe link in an email), the version of the wording the App showed you, and the App language. We keep a copy of the exact wording of each version, so we can show what you agreed to.
  • If you opt in, we give Brevo your email address, your App language, and where and on which date you opted in, so it can send you the emails. If you opt out, we remove your address from the list for parking tips.

f) Sign in with Apple

If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email address, we send emails to your Apple relay address through Apple's Private Email Relay service, which forwards them to you.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your data to third parties. However, we use third-party services to provide functionality:

  • Microsoft Azure (Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Vision, for AI-based recognition and processing of the parking sign images you upload)
  • Google Cloud and Firebase (for running our server and storing your account data, scan records and uploaded images)
  • Clerk (for user authentication and account management, including sign-in emails)
  • RevenueCat (for in-app purchases of credits)
  • Brevo (EU-based email delivery service, for sending service messages and, if you opted in, parking tips by email)
  • Apple (for Sign in with Apple, its Private Email Relay service, and purchases in the App Store)
  • Google (for Sign in with Google and purchases in Google Play)

Microsoft, Google Cloud and Firebase, Clerk, RevenueCat and Brevo process personal data on our behalf, only to provide their services to us. When you use Sign in with Apple or Google, or buy credits in the App Store or Google Play, Apple and Google also handle your data under their own privacy policies.

5. International Data Transfers

We choose locations in the European Union (EU) where we can. Our server runs on Google Cloud in Finland, and the Microsoft Azure services that read your images run in data centres in the EU. Some of our providers are based in the United States or use subprocessors outside the EU and the European Economic Area (EEA), so some personal data is processed there:

  • Clerk (United States): your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • RevenueCat (United States): your account ID and your purchase history.
  • Google and Microsoft (both based in the United States): they may process some data outside the EU, for example for support or security.
  • Brevo (based in France): some of the subprocessors it uses to deliver email may be outside the EU/EEA.
  • Apple and Google, when you use their sign-in or buy credits in their stores, under their own privacy policies.

When we transfer personal data outside the EU/EEA, it is protected by one of these safeguards: the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for US companies certified under it, or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us which safeguard applies to a provider, and for a copy of it, at app-support@sparkling-ai.com.

6. Data Storage & Retention

  • Uploaded images are stored temporarily for up to 12 months and then automatically deleted.
  • User account data is retained until the account is deleted or terminated.
  • Transaction/payment history is kept until the user account is deleted or terminated.
  • Your communication preferences and their history are kept until the account is deleted. When you delete your account, we also delete your contact details from our email delivery service, Brevo.
  • Where we store and process your data, and how it is protected when it is processed outside the EU, is described in section 5.

7. User Rights (GDPR Compliance)

As a user in the EU, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your stored personal data.
  • Right to Correction: Update incorrect or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion: Delete your account via the app, which will erase all associated data.
  • Right to Object: You cannot opt out of AI model training or analytics, as they are essential to the App's functionality.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in a structured format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent, such as for parking tips by email, you can withdraw it at any time using the unsubscribe link in any such email or the switch in the App's Settings.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you think we handle your personal data unlawfully, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), www.imy.se, or to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work.

To exercise these rights, contact us at app-support@sparkling-ai.com.

8. Data Security Measures

We take the security of your data seriously. Measures include:

  • Encryption of stored data to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Secure authentication for user accounts via Clerk.
  • Restricted data access for authorized personnel only.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 15 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 15.

10. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated, and the latest version is always available in the App and on our website. If we make a significant change to how we use personal data you have already given us, we will tell you by email or in the App before the change applies to your data. A new optional feature that needs your consent, such as parking tips by email, uses your data only after you turn it on in the App.
