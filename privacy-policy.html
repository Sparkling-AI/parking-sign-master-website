Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-11-06

Welcome to Parking Sign Master (Sweden), an application provided by Sparkling AI AB, a company registered in Sweden ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use the Parking Sign Master (Sweden) app ("App"). Please read it to understand how we handle your data and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of data when you use the App:

a) Contact Information

  • Name (Used for App Functionality)
  • Email Address (Used for App Functionality, service messages, and, only if you opt in, email communications)

b) Usage Data

  • Product Interaction (Used for Analytics & App Functionality)

c) Diagnostics Data

  • Crash Data (Used for Analytics & App Functionality)

d) Uploaded Data

  • Images of Parking Signs (Used for AI model improvement, feature testing, and bug fixes; stored temporarily for up to 12 months)

e) Communication Preferences

  • Your email choice (whether you opted in to email communications, when, and which version of the request you answered; used to respect and document your choice)
  • App language (used to send emails in your language)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide core functionality of the App, including processing parking sign images.
  • To improve AI models by using uploaded images to enhance sign recognition.
  • To process in-app purchases and manage credit-based transactions.
  • To analyze user behavior and enhance user experience via Firebase Analytics.
  • To handle user authentication and account management via Clerk.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions via RevenueCat.
  • To ensure app stability by collecting crash data and diagnosing technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse (e.g., preventing exploitation of free credits).
  • To send service messages about your account, security, and important changes to this Privacy Policy or our Terms of Use.
  • To send parking tips and holiday reminders by email, only if you have opted in.

3. Email Communications and Notifications

a) Service Messages

We may email all users about their account, security, or important changes to this Privacy Policy or our Terms of Use. These messages are not marketing. We send them because they are necessary to provide the App and to keep you informed.

b) Parking Tips and Holiday Emails (Opt-In)

  • We send parking tips and reminders before holidays and travel periods only if you opt in. The choice is off by default, and you can make it when you sign up, in a one-time prompt in the App, or in the App's Settings.
  • The legal basis for these emails is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time, free of charge, using the unsubscribe link in every email or the switch in the App's Settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect emails sent before.
  • We keep a record of your choice (what you chose, when, and which version of the request you saw) so we can respect it and demonstrate it.

c) Notifications on Your Device

  • Holiday parking reminders and parking time reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The information used to schedule them, such as the time by which you need to move your vehicle, stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • These notifications need your permission in your device's settings. You can turn them off at any time in the App's Settings or in your device's settings.

d) Sign in with Apple

If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email address, we send emails to your Apple relay address through Apple's Private Email Relay service, which forwards them to you.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your data to third parties. However, we use third-party services to provide functionality:

  • Azure OpenAI API (for AI-based recognition and processing)
  • Google APIs (for authentication and location-based services)
  • Firebase Analytics (for app usage tracking and diagnostics)
  • Clerk (for user authentication and account management)
  • RevenueCat (for in-app purchases and subscription management)
  • Brevo (EU-based email delivery service, for sending service messages and, if you opted in, parking tips and holiday emails)
  • Apple (for Sign in with Apple and its Private Email Relay service)

These third-party providers process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Retention

  • Uploaded images are stored temporarily for up to 12 months and then automatically deleted.
  • User account data is retained until the account is deleted or terminated.
  • Transaction/payment history is kept until the user account is deleted or terminated.
  • Your communication preferences are kept until the account is deleted. When you delete your account, we also remove your contact details from our email delivery service.
  • While some data is processed and stored within the European Union (EU), certain data may be processed outside the EU by third-party providers such as Clerk and RevenueCat. These providers follow their respective data protection policies and apply necessary safeguards.

6. User Rights (GDPR Compliance)

As a user in the EU, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your stored personal data.
  • Right to Correction: Update incorrect or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion: Delete your account via the app, which will erase all associated data.
  • Right to Object: You cannot opt out of AI model training or analytics, as they are essential to the App's functionality.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in a structured format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent, such as for parking tips and holiday emails, you can withdraw it at any time using the unsubscribe link in any such email or in the App's Settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at app-support@sparkling-ai.com.

7. Data Security Measures

We take the security of your data seriously. Measures include:

  • Encryption of stored data to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Secure authentication for user accounts via Clerk.
  • Restricted data access for authorized personnel only.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 15 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 15.

9. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version is always available in the App and on our website. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by email or in the App before they take effect.
